# DATA_LOG

Every change to an input number and why.

## 2026-11-22

- Created all input files with assumption-based values. Nothing is sourced yet except the diesel emission factor (IPCC 2006), the base carbon price (UU HPP minimum rate) and the discount rate and safety threshold (taken from the upstream Kopibara engine).
- `wave_climate.csv`: generated by `scripts/fetch_waves.py` in placeholder mode (no Copernicus credentials). The reanalysis branch of the script has not been run.
- `routes.csv`, Ternate - Sofifi `current_vessels`: 1 to 2. With 1 vessel today's fleet could carry only about half of the assumed demand, which made the baseline unrealistic.
- `routes.csv`, Tobelo - Daruba `daily_pax`: 450 to 420. Same reason: today's single vessel was 5 percent short of the assumed demand.
- `vessel_types.csv`, electric `kwh_per_nm`: 60 to 45. The first value was not consistent with the diesel archetype (about 14 L per nm at 12 knots, about 40 percent engine efficiency, scaled to 10 knots gives about 40 to 45 kWh per nm).
